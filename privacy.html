Privacy policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026 · Developer: Song Li

简体中文 ↓

The SweepZen app

SweepZen processes file and application information on your Mac. The app does not collect or transmit your files, file paths, scan results, usage activity, device identifiers, or personal information to the developer. It has no account system, ads, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking.

SweepZen has no network client entitlement and does not provide a file-upload service.

Information used locally

When you request a scan, space map, or application review, SweepZen reads information such as file names and paths, sizes and dates, application metadata, and running-application information to explain disk use and protect files in use. Some application configuration and provenance files are read locally to identify what data belongs to.

Folder access is requested through macOS. SweepZen stores security-scoped folder bookmarks and app preferences locally, including the selected language and the date of a completed cleanup. Scan results and selections are kept in memory during use. A cleanup list is saved to a location you choose only when you export it.

Files you choose to remove are moved to macOS Trash. SweepZen does not automatically empty Trash. macOS may restrict access to some files even after you grant folder access.

Support messages

If you email the developer, the email service processes your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue, and is retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send private files, passwords, or unredacted sensitive screenshots.

GitHub issues are public and are processed by GitHub under its own privacy statement. You may use email instead of opening a public issue. Contact the developer if you would like a support email deleted, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website and Apple services

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

App Store purchases, downloads, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled by Apple under its own policies and settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact Song Li at songlaoshi666@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日 · 开发者：Song Li

SweepZen 应用

SweepZen 在你的 Mac 本地处理文件和应用信息，不向开发者收集或传输文件、文件路径、扫描结果、使用记录、设备标识符或个人信息。应用无需账户，不含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或追踪功能，也不提供文件上传服务。

本地使用的信息

当你主动扫描、查看空间分布或检查应用时，SweepZen 会读取文件名、路径、大小、日期、应用元数据与正在运行的应用信息，用于解释空间占用和保护正在使用的文件。应用会在本地读取部分配置及来源信息，以判断数据归属。

文件夹访问由 macOS 授权。授权书签、语言偏好与完成清理的日期保存在本机；扫描结果和选择在使用期间保存在内存中。只有在你主动导出清单时，才会在你选择的位置保存清单。

你选择移除的文件会进入 macOS 废纸篓，SweepZen 不会自动清空。即使已授予文件夹访问权限，macOS 仍可能限制某些文件的访问。

支持沟通

如果你发送支持邮件，邮件服务会处理你提供的邮箱地址、正文和附件。这些信息用于回复与排查问题，仅在支持工作及适用义务所需的期限内保留。请勿发送密码、私人文件或未遮挡的敏感截图。你可以联系开发者申请删除支持邮件，但仍可能存在适用的保留义务。

GitHub 问题反馈公开可见，并适用 GitHub 的隐私声明；你也可以通过邮件联系，而不公开发布问题。

网站和 Apple 服务

本支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages，不添加分析服务、广告、追踪 Cookie 或第三方字体。GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息，以提供并保护托管服务，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。

App Store 购买、下载以及你选择分享给 Apple 的诊断信息，由 Apple 按其政策和你的设置处理，详见 Apple 隐私政策。

更新与联系

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本页面会更新生效日期。隐私问题请联系开发者 Song Li：songlaoshi666@gmail.com。
