Getting started

  1. Open SweepZen on a Mac running macOS 14 or later.
  2. Choose Scan and clear, Space map, or Uninstall applications.
  3. When prompted, select your home folder and click Grant Access. Access is remembered locally for your next session.
  4. Review the findings, search or filter the list, and open details before selecting items.
  5. Review the confirmation list before moving anything to Trash.

Common questions

Why hasn’t free space increased?

Moving files to Trash does not usually free their disk space. Review the files in Finder and empty Trash yourself when you are sure. Local snapshots and shared APFS storage can also delay or reduce the space returned. SweepZen never empties Trash for you.

How do I recover a file?

Open Trash in Finder. Use Put Back if available, or move the file to its original location. Recovery through Trash is available only until you empty it.

Why was an item skipped?

SweepZen skips protected locations, files associated with running apps, shared application data, and paths outside the folder you granted access to. Quit the relevant application and scan again if appropriate.

Folder access stopped working. What should I do?

Return to the home screen and choose Grant Folder Access. Select your home folder again. macOS may still restrict protected areas; SweepZen cannot bypass those restrictions.

Why does uninstalling need another folder grant?

Applications in /Applications are outside your home folder. macOS requires a separate grant before SweepZen can move them to Trash. You can cancel the request.

Does SweepZen upload my files?

No. The app processes file information locally and does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or an upload service. See the privacy policy for details, including website hosting and support messages.

简体中文

看清空间，再决定清理哪些。

SweepZen 帮你查看应用缓存、构建产物和文件夹的空间占用，并在移除应用前检查关联数据。支持 macOS 14 及以上版本。

开始使用

  1. 打开应用，选择「扫描清理」「空间透视」或「卸载应用」。
  2. macOS 提示授权时，选择你的个人文件夹，点击「授予访问」。授权会保存在这台 Mac 上。
  3. 查看结果，按应用、文件名或路径搜索；可筛选可清理项或已选项。
  4. 展开详情，了解用途和移除后果，再勾选项目。
  5. 检查确认清单后，将所选项目移入废纸篓。

空间没有立即释放？

移入废纸篓通常不会立即释放磁盘空间。请先在访达中确认文件，再自行清空废纸篓。APFS 共享存储和本地快照可能使实际释放空间少于文件大小。SweepZen 不会自动清空废纸篓。

如何恢复文件？

在访达中打开废纸篓，使用可用的「放回原处」操作，或把文件移回原来的文件夹。清空废纸篓后，无法再通过废纸篓恢复。

授权失效或项目被跳过？

返回首页，选择「授予文件夹访问权限」重新授权个人文件夹。系统保护的数据、正在使用的文件、共享数据以及超出授权范围的内容会被保留。必要时退出对应应用，再重新扫描。
