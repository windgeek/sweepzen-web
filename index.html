Help & support

A little clarity for your Mac.

Understand what takes up space, review what can go, and keep the decision in your hands.

01 / SCAN

Review before clearing

Scan app caches and build output. Each finding explains what it is and what removing it changes.

02 / EXPLORE

Find the larger picture

Explore the storage map by category, then follow folders to find what takes up the most space.

03 / UNINSTALL

Choose what goes

Review apps and their related files. Shared data stays protected. Items removed in SweepZen go to Trash.

Getting started

  1. Open SweepZen on a Mac running macOS 14 or later.
  2. Choose Scan and clear, Storage map, or Uninstall. Background & Extensions and the toolbox provide separate review and troubleshooting tools.
  3. When macOS asks, grant access to your home folder. Uninstalling apps or reviewing temporary files may require separate folder access. Grants are remembered locally.
  4. Review each finding and its details. Select what to remove; shared data and items whose ownership cannot be confirmed stay protected.
  5. Check the confirmation list before moving selected items to Trash.

Common questions

Why hasn’t free space increased?

Moving files to Trash usually does not free their disk space. Check the files in Finder and empty Trash yourself when you are sure. APFS shared storage and local snapshots can affect the space returned. SweepZen never empties Trash or deletes Time Machine snapshots.

How do I recover a file?

Open Trash in Finder. Use Put Back if available, or move the file to its original location. Recovery through Trash is available only until you empty it.

Why was an item skipped or kept?

Protected files, shared data, and data that another app copy may still use are kept. Copies outside Applications also count. Leftovers held by another copy are shown with a location and reason, and cannot be selected for removal. If the app cannot confirm ownership or access, it keeps the item.

How do I renew folder access?

Open SweepZen’s Settings and grant access to the relevant folder again. Use your home folder for scanning. macOS may still restrict some locations; SweepZen cannot bypass those restrictions.

Why does uninstalling ask for access to Applications?

Applications needs separate folder access. If SweepZen asks for it, choose Grant Applications folder. Folder access does not provide administrator privileges. For an administrator-installed app, use Show in Finder, move the app to Trash and authenticate if macOS asks; then return to SweepZen to review its data. App Management and Full Disk Access are separate system permissions; use the relevant settings link only when the app prompts for it.

Can SweepZen remove system-level items?

Items requiring administrator authorization can be revealed in Finder, but SweepZen cannot remove them. Toolbox commands explain their effect and cost. You copy and run them yourself; SweepZen never runs administrator commands.

How do background items and Finder menus work?

SweepZen shows background apps, extensions, and the origins of Finder menu entries. Follow the supplied macOS settings or service-stop instructions when needed. You can choose the default app for common file types directly in SweepZen. These tools do not automatically delete system menu entries.

Does SweepZen upload my files?

No. The app processes file information locally, makes no network requests, and has no accounts, ads, analytics, or upload service. The privacy policy separately explains website hosting and support messages.

I stopped a background program. Why is removal still blocked?

An app may have several helpers, or a helper may restart. Save your work, expand the stop instructions, copy the search name and find it in Activity Monitor. Advanced Terminal instructions are optional. SweepZen checks the selected app every 2 seconds and when you return; choose Check again if needed. If another helper remains, its current name is shown. Stopping a helper does not start removal: review the page and confirm before continuing.

Must I check Login Items & Extensions before uninstalling?

Usually no. Shipping a login component does not prove it is enabled. After removal, check settings only if the entry still appears or you want to change startup behavior. A listed entry alone does not mean the app is running. macOS manages the list; emptying Trash does not guarantee that the entry immediately disappears. Use the available switch or remove button in settings if needed.
